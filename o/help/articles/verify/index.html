1. Getting Your Unique Key

First things first, you will need to execute Overdrive H and get the key. If you do not know how to get the Key, you can see this helpful Tutorial. After all that, you will want to click on your Roblox profile picture on the upper left corner of the screen.

2. Verifying Yourself

Once you have your Unique Identifier, you will want to join our Discord Server and go to the #「🔐」panel channel and click the Green Login Button

Once you have done that, a screen/pop-up should appear. You will have to paste your unique code into the "Enter Unique Identifier" textbox and confirm it.

WARNING: MAKE SURE YOUR INVENTORY IS PUBLIC! IF YOU DO NOT KNOW OR WANT TO MAKE YOUR INVENTORY PUBLIC, GO TO ROBLOX'S PRIVACY SETTINGS!

3. Last Steps

Once you successfully verified, you want to click the Copy Script blue button.

Once done, this message should appear. Just accept our Terms of Service (we recommend reading them so your access isn't terminated).

And now, you should have your script. Copy it, join the game you want, and have fun! :D
